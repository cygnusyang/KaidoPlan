How do I export a delivery report for a client?

A delivery report is not a screenshot of a task list. It answers three questions in order: what is the date, what decides it, and what changed since the last report.

Updated 2026-11-08 · by cygnus, the developer of KaidoPlan

What belongs in the report

  1. The computed finish date, not the date someone hoped for.
  2. The critical path — the chain that produces that date, named by task.
  3. Violations, including ones that are still open. A report that hides an open conflict is a report that will be contradicted next week.
  4. Variance against a baseline: planned versus actual per milestone, so "on track" is a measurement.

Exporting

In the app, the delivery report is exported from the workbench. The free tier allows three exports per month with unlimited preview; Pro removes the limit. The preview is the part that matters most — you can verify the numbers before you send anything.

If you want the same content without a document, the numbers come from the CLI:

kaidoplan schedule compute --file plan.kaido
kaidoplan schedule path --file plan.kaido
kaidoplan violations --file plan.kaido
kaidoplan baseline variance --file plan.kaido

The habit that makes reports cheap

Take a baseline at the start of each reporting period. Then the report is a comparison — plan against actual — instead of a narrative written from memory. It also removes the most common argument in a delivery review: whether the date "changed" or was always wrong.

Keep the plan in the repository and the report has a source you can point at. That is a stronger position than a slide deck nobody can audit.

Questions this page does not answer

Can I export to PDF or CSV?

The app's delivery report export is the supported path. The plan file is plain JSON, so any other format is a short script away if you need one.

Why is there an export limit on the free tier?

Because the recurring use of a schedule is producing delivery reports. Planning itself, including the critical path and validation, stays free.
